# AI advisor demo: Bauman, 4 queries without a citation (2026-11-19)

Done **by hand** on the pilot's stored real responses (`tests/fixtures/real/watcher-bauman-pilot-2026-11-19/`) and Bauman's public site. It shows what an advisor would add before building one (D-014, D-044). **It is one day and these are hypotheses**: AI answers change.

## "how to sell rare books" and "where to sell first edition books" (one opportunity)
- **What the AI said:** identify the edition, assess condition, look at sold prices (not asking prices) and pick a channel: dealer (fast) or marketplace/auction (more money, slower). In AI Mode: antiquarian shops buy at 25–30% of resale value or take consignment.
- **Whom it cited:** forums and YouTube (reddit, youtube, justanswer, an ecwid site and a 50webs one); in AI Mode, abebooks and nembol. Weak sources: the bar to beat is low.
- **What Bauman has:** only `selling-something.aspx`, a form to offer books (564 words). There is no "how to sell" guide.
- **Recommendation:** a "How to sell rare books" guide that follows the AI's order (edition, condition, sold prices, channel) and explains how Bauman buys and consigns, from a dealer's point of view. "People also ask" questions as an FAQ and a link to the form. One page attacks both queries.

## "most valuable stephen king books"
- **What the AI said:** concrete figures (for example the first five books signed collection ≈ USD 59,757; the lettered Firestarter ≈ USD 23,867).
- **Whom it cited:** litreactor, reddit, facebook.
- **What Bauman has:** the post "Collecting Stephen King: Six Early Books" (1,535 words) with **a single price**.
- **Recommendation:** turn it into "Most valuable Stephen King books" with a table (title, edition, year, price range) using **only real values** from Bauman's stock or sales (first-party evidence litreactor lacks); link to the King catalog pages and add how to recognize a first edition.

## "gifts for a book lover"
- **What the AI said:** a shopping-style answer with products and prices (LEGO, mugs, reading lights).
- **Reading:** the intent is cheap, mass gifts; Bauman sells rare books. **It is the wrong fight.** Replace the query with closer ones ("rare book gift ideas", gifts by budget). Also, the LLM Mentions database listed it as cited and today it is not (it is a snapshot).

## Limits
